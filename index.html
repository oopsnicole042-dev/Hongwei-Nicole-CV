Profile个人简介

PhD candidate in Auditing at Zhongnan University of Economics and Law. My research evaluates regulatory and policy interventions with quasi-experimental methods (staggered difference-in-differences, event studies) using hand-collected and large-scale firm data. I trained as an accountant in Australia and worked at a Melbourne accounting firm on reconciliations, accounts payable, supplier records and tax-authority liaison. I work in Chinese and English and have studied, worked and volunteered in multicultural settings.
